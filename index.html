Five-a-side · two referees · no morals

FOUL PLAY FC

Score goals or win the crowd. Foul anyone you can reach, on your team or theirs. The referees only see what's inside their yellow cones, so throw sand in their eyes first. Play well and the crowd throws you bats, flash powder, bananas and med kits.

  • Foul: hits the nearest player in reach (red ring). Cycle to choose another.
  • Caught? Yellow card and a free kick. Second yellow or a bat foul: red, off for 25s.
  • Sand: blinds the nearest ref for 4.5s. Recharges every 22s. The other ref may still see you.
  • Crowd meter: goals, tackles, saves and unseen fouls fill it. Full meter = a gift from the stands.
  • Charge: fills with possession and good play. Spend it to sprint; any shot at 25% or more becomes an accurate power shot.
  • Karma: fouls lower it and make bots come after you. Help a downed player up to raise it. Referees let trusted players off and ignore fouls on serial offenders.

Match format

5v5 plays on a pitch with four times the area of 3v3, marked like a full-size pitch. Applies from the next match; online, the host's choice is used.

Graphics

3D uses WebGL and loads three.js the first time. Classic is the original 2.5D view and runs anywhere.

Game pace

Slows players, ball, referees and bots together. The match clock still runs in real time.

Match length

Applies from the next match. Online, the host's setting is used.
