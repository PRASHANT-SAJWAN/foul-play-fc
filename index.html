Five-a-side · two referees · no morals

FOUL PLAY FC

Score goals or win the crowd. Foul anyone you can reach, on your team or theirs. The referees only see what's inside their yellow cones, so throw sand in their eyes first. Play well and the crowd throws you bats, flash powder, bananas and med kits.

  • Foul: hits the nearest player in reach (red ring). Cycle to choose another.
  • Caught? Yellow card and a free kick. Second yellow or a bat foul: red, off for 25s.
  • Sand: blinds the nearest ref for 4.5s. Recharges every 22s. The other ref may still see you.
  • Crowd meter: goals, tackles, saves and unseen fouls fill it. Full meter = a gift from the stands.
