Small-sided · one referee · no morals

FOUL PLAY FC

Score goals or win the crowd. Foul anyone you can reach, on your team or theirs. The referee only sees what's inside the yellow cone, so throw sand in their eyes first. Play well and the crowd throws you med kits, swords, rocket boots, ultimate charges and fake whistles.

  • Foul: hits the nearest player in reach (red ring). Cycle to choose another.
  • Caught? Yellow card and a free kick. Second yellow or a bat foul: red, off for 25s.
  • Sand: blinds the referee for 4.5s if they're in reach. Recharges every 22s. Nobody calls fouls while they're blinded.
  • Crowd meter: goals, tackles, saves and unseen fouls fill it. Full meter = a gift from the stands.
  • Charge: fills with possession and good play. Spend it to sprint; any shot at 25% or more becomes an accurate power shot.
  • Karma: fouls lower it and make bots come after you. Help a downed player up to raise it. The referee lets trusted players off and ignores fouls on serial offenders.
